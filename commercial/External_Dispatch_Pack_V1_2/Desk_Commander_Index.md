# Desk Commander Index

This is the external dispatch pack for the Hyperplasticity Protocol (HPP V2).

## Your Role

Use these materials to route public, investor, and technical-review conversations without exposing repo internals or mixing unrelated project tracks.

## Navigation

1. **Samurai_Addendum.zip**  
   Public-safe signal pack: LinkedIn drafts, founder note, reply snippets, and internal wording guardrails.

2. **HPP_Governance.zip**  
   Sanitized HPP V2 governance summary for technical reviewers or buyers under the right discussion context.

3. **Email_Snippets.md**  
   Short outreach templates for opening doors without attaching technical material too early.

4. **Smoke_Reports.md**  
   Summary of the public-copy smoke test and dispatch-pack constraints.

## Use Rules

- Cold or nontechnical contact: send no zip first; offer MNDA or a short call.
- Technical reviewer under MNDA: send the appropriate zip plus the manifest.
- HQA inquiries: do not send HPP materials as an HQA response. HQA is separate and not included in this packet.

The signs are on the doors. Route carefully.
