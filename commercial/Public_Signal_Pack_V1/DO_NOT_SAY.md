# DO NOT SAY: Banned Language & Concepts

This is an internal checklist of vocabulary and concepts that are strictly banned from all public-facing copy, networking material, and investor pitches.

## 1. Absolute / Overclaim Words
- **BANNED:** "Guarantee"
- **BANNED:** "Unhackable"
- **BANNED:** "Impossible"
- **BANNED:** "Definitive Proof"
- **BANNED:** "Liability-Free"
*Reason:* These words create massive legal exposure and invite bad actors to challenge the system. Use "highly governed," "structurally verified," and "mathematically aligned."

## 2. AGI / Consciousness Claims
- **BANNED:** "AGI" or "Artificial General Intelligence"
- **BANNED:** "Sentience" or "Sentient"
- **BANNED:** "Consciousness" or "Self-Aware"
*Reason:* The industry immediately discredits these claims. We are building a *deterministic, structurally anchored* protocol, not a living entity. 

## 3. Robotics / Hardware Bleed
- **BANNED:** "Masamune"
- **BANNED:** "Live Robotics Integrations"
- **BANNED:** "Bio-kill switches"
- **BANNED:** "Drone Fleet"
*Reason:* The Hyperplasticity Protocol (HPP) is being presented purely as a neural software architecture. Do not cross the streams with physical security or robotics deployments.

## 4. Architecture Bleed
- **BANNED:** "HQA" or "Hyperplasticity Quantum Architecture"
- **BANNED:** Mentioning topological error correction tied to HQA.
*Reason:* HQA and CUDA-Q integrations are highly classified internal tracks. Keep HPP focused strictly on the Neuro-Structural imperative (myelination, anchoring, RDT).

## 5. Local Paths
- **BANNED:** References to `Z:\` or `X:\` drives.
*Reason:* Operational Security.
